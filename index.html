FAQ
Will it actually sound like me? +
Yes — that's the entire point. We spend time studying your existing content, your vocabulary, your tone, and your stories before we write a single word. Most clients can't tell the difference between what we write and what they'd write themselves.
How much time do I need to put in? +
Minimal. For LinkedIn we need a 15-30 minute interview once a week. For newsletters we need about 20 minutes of your thoughts. For scripts, we need a brief and any reference material. We handle everything else.
Do you use AI to write the content? +
We use AI as a tool but every piece of content goes through a human editing pass. LinkedIn's algorithm actively suppresses raw AI content so we make sure everything reads like a real person wrote it — because a real person did review it.
What results can I expect? +
Results depend on your industry and audience, but consistent posting on LinkedIn typically leads to more profile views, inbound connection requests, and direct messages from potential clients within 30-60 days. One DTC founder closed a $1.2M partnership in his first year from LinkedIn ghostwriting alone.
Can I approve content before it goes live? +
Always. Nothing goes live without your approval. We deliver drafts ahead of schedule so you have time to review, request changes, or approve. You stay in full control of your brand.
